2. No Account, No Personal Identity

The App does not ask you to register. On first launch it signs you in anonymously through Firebase Authentication, which issues a random identifier (for example nRvpC1SnSPUYAANdK6…). That identifier is how your saved plants are grouped together.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, precise location, contacts, calendar, health data, or advertising identifiers.

Important consequence of anonymous accounts: because the identifier lives only on your device, if you delete the App, reset the device, or lose it, we have no way to recognise you again and your cloud backup cannot be recovered or transferred. There is no password reset, because there is no password.

3. Information You Provide

3.1 Photos of plants

When you scan a plant, you either take a photo with the camera or choose an existing one from your photo library. That single image is uploaded to our server function and forwarded to Google's Gemini AI for analysis. See section 5 for details.

The App accesses only the specific image you select. It does not browse, index, or upload your photo library.

3.2 Plant records you save

If you tap "Save to My plants", the App stores:

  • the photo;
  • the AI result: common name, botanical (Latin) name, toxicity indication, watering, light, temperature and fertilizer notes, and tags;
  • the date the record was created;
  • your watering reminder settings and the date you last marked the plant as watered, if you set a reminder;
  • any nickname or note you add.

5. How the AI Feature Works

This is the part of the App that involves sending your content to a third party, so we describe it plainly.

  1. You choose or take a photo.
  2. The App resizes it and sends it to our own server function, hosted on Google Cloud Functions in the europe-west1 region.
  3. Our function checks that you are within your scan allowance, then forwards the image to the Google Gemini API together with a text instruction asking it to identify the plant, or to assess its health when you use the AI Doctor feature.
  4. Gemini returns a text result. Our function passes that result back to the App.

Google processes the image as a data processor for that request under Google's own terms and privacy documentation. We do not control Google's internal processing. Please review Google's Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms.

We do not use your photos or plant records to train any model of our own.

Please do not scan anything private. The feature is designed for photographs of plants. Avoid including people, faces, documents, screens, or anything else you would not want processed by a third-party AI service.

6. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device. Your plant list lives in a local database on the device and works offline. This is the primary copy.
  • In our cloud. Saved plants are also copied to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore for the text, Cloud Storage for the photos) under your anonymous identifier, so that the list survives if the App is closed unexpectedly and so it can be restored on the same device.

Access is enforced by server-side security rules: a request carrying your anonymous identifier can read and write only data filed under that same identifier. All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS.

7. Subscriptions and Payments

Planty AI offers an optional auto-renewing subscription ("Planty AI Pro") that removes the daily scan limit and unlocks additional features.

We never see or handle your card details. Payment is processed entirely by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to verify purchases and tell the App whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your anonymous identifier and the purchase receipt from the store.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

9. Device Permissions

  • Camera — only to photograph a plant when you choose the camera. Nothing is recorded in the background.
  • Photo library — only to let you pick an existing photo. The App reads the single image you select.
  • Notifications — only to deliver the watering reminders you set up. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

You can withdraw any of these permissions at any time in your device settings. The rest of the App keeps working.

10. Data Retention

Saved plants are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting a plant in the App removes it from your device and from our cloud, including its photo.

Technical logs are kept for a short period for troubleshooting and cost monitoring, then deleted automatically.

11. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete a single plant — press and hold it in My Plants, or open it and choose Delete.
  • Delete everything — Settings → Delete Account. This erases your saved plants, their photos, your usage counters, and your anonymous account itself. It cannot be undone.
  • Cancel your subscription — in your App Store or Google Play settings. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; you must cancel it in the store.
  • Ask us — write to fazogirapps@gmail.com. Because accounts are anonymous, we may be unable to locate your data unless you can supply your anonymous identifier.
