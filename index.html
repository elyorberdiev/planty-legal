LeafScout

Legal documents for the LeafScout mobile app (com.fazogir.plantai), available on iOS and Android.
Last Updated: August 14, 2026

LeafScout identifies plants from a photograph using artificial intelligence, suggests how to care for them, and reminds you when to water. It works without an account: you are signed in anonymously, and no email, name, or password is ever collected.

Please note: identifications, toxicity indications, and health assessments are generated by an AI model and can be wrong. Never rely on the App to decide whether a plant is safe to touch, eat, or keep near children or pets. See section 3 of the Terms of Use.
